### S67 · The price rule: derivation, time step and wording (may take 2 sessions)
Added Nov 9 (Chat audit; `audits/2026-11-09-simulation-audit.md`, finding 2). S63 files no main-row decision on the price rule before this step reports.
1. Correction notes, dated, no figure changed: in compassionism-simulation `dev/reports/s58-price-rule.md` ("In plain words" item 2 and section 8's Hub sentence), the one-for-one long-run pass-through is built into the rule (its state is a money-implied level that prices close on; with steady money growth, any κ > 0 and α + ρ < 1, steady-state inflation equals money growth), so it is an assumption shared with today's rule and supported by McCandless and Weber, not a result of the pilot; in `dev/reports/s57-distribution-measures.md`, the sentence that real wealth falls "not because wealth is destroyed" (for a holder of nominal savings it is; the model treats all wealth as nominal).
2. The closed form in MODEL_SPEC (with S68): inflation follows x_t = (1 + φ − κ)x_{t−1} − φx_{t−2} + κm with φ = α + ρ; a table of modulus and period for every swept reading (main values: 0.885 and 8.8 years), checked against the engine's yearly path.
3. κ on the rule's own variable: the price-gap (P-star) form on US data (M2, trend velocity, potential output; open Hallman, Porter and Small, *American Economic Review* 1991 first), beside today's output-gap regression; standard errors robust to serial correlation; subsample values printed (today's are unstable: persistence 0.92 in 1960-1983 against 0.49 in 1984-2024; the slope below potential 0.66 against −0.21).
4. Dynamics from high inflation: a panel of country-years with sustained inflation above 20% a year (World Bank or IMF annual consumer prices and broad money, opened and hashed): persistence, speed of adjustment, and whether inflation runs ahead of money growth (velocity; open Cagan 1956 first). Readings, never targets.
5. Two readings behind `PRICE_RULE` options (off = identical): a within-year price step (quarterly, no random draw), to test whether the waves and the within-year shortage (22.5% and 11.3% of demand in S58) survive a shorter lag; and a velocity response, so pass-through can exceed 1 under sustained money.
6. Pilot at seeds 1-100 in three environments; unit tests for each reading alone; then the recommendation for S63's main-row decision, written with both views: the S58 preregistration's (the capacity rule as the main row) and the audit's (today's rule as the main row, the capacity rule beside it, every wealth outcome as a yearly average under both, because the cycle is a property of the annual lag structure and of parameters that are not stable).

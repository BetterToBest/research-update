## Defect 1: the ESP payroll pool with no wage weight (the BU stock identity)
- **What failed.** The dry run `s93dry-69e8953` failed the framework BU stock identity (`bu`, index.html `acctYearEnd`) in r2 part F's least-favourable bound points (points 116-125; every cell) and in the supply study's rows 47-48 at 40 years.
- **How it was found.** In a scratch copy of the engine, `acctYearEnd` logged the identity's pieces when it failed (part F point 116, Reference, 40 years, seeds 1-2). Each year's gap equalled the ESP's payroll pool of that year (for example year 23: gap 4.0642e20, pool 4.0642e20), while the counted conversions (`esRet`) were 0. A second log, at the pool's allocation (index.html, ESP block, `ESS.wW=esW`), showed 90-98 ESP workers with a wage weight of 0 in every failing year (`esW = 0`). With no weight, `sh = esPool*w/esW` is never computed, so the pool is neither shared nor returned.
- **Every failure explained.** All 39 failing years of part F point 116 and all 5 of supply rows 47-48 (Reference, 40 years, seeds 1-2) have a gap equal to a zero-weight pool, to within 1e-6.
- **Fix** (v5.9.23): when the pool is positive and no ESP worker had a wage, the whole pool is returned to the ESP (`esRet`, tallied in `retWage` and a new reporting-only `retNoW`).
- **Proofs on the fixed engine:**
  - Part F Reference 40 years, points 110-125, seeds 1-2: every run passes. The 11 unreached points equal the dry run's output exactly; only points 116, 120, 123, 124 and 125 change.
  - Dry-run units that passed, rerun and compared with `compare-shards.js`, all EQUAL: part M Reference 20 years (11,916 values), part S Reference 40 years (18,920), part B Reference 20 years (5,958) and the yardstick study Reference 20 years (43,654).
  - Guard test identical (3 environments x 25 rows x 20 seeds x 483 outputs), locally and in CI. Lineage panels: same fingerprints as v5.9.22. Validate, unit (424) and domtest (162) pass.

## Defect 2: the household transfer check's scale (reporting only)
- **What failed.** The scan of all 57 dry-run units for any failing identity also found the household transfer check (`household`) failing four times, all in part F at Adverse, 40 years (bound points 119, 123, 124). The earlier record named only the BU identity.
- **Cause.** Each failure is a couple whose debt had run away (1.8e23 to 8.5e36 dollars) and was reset to the wealth floor ($10,000). The pooling's two transfers differ by exactly one rounding step (ulp) of that debt: 2^25, 2^28, 2^56 and 2^70, a relative error of 1.4-2.1e-16. The check measured its scale on the wealth after the floor, so that rounding step looked like a failure.
- **Fix:** the scale also counts the amount the floor removed (`_acFl`). No figure reads the accounts.
- **Proofs:** part F Adverse 40 years, bound points 116-125, passes every check. Lineage reproved (fingerprints equal v5.9.22's). Domtest 162.
- No unit of the dry run failed any other identity.

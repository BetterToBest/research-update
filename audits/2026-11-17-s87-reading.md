# An outside reading of "Money, metals and wages in the US, 1792-2025" (S87, Nov 17, 2026)

Before publishing the Hub page `us-money-history.html` (deep history, part 2), its draft was read by a language model from a different maker, as the plan asks. A model's reading is a check, never a source: nothing it says enters a figure unless the source is opened.

- **Model:** `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b` on NVIDIA's API catalogue (the largest general model in its list on Nov 17), temperature 0.2.
- **What it was given:** the page's rendered text with every table opened, the sources file (`data/s87/SOURCES.md`) and the minimum-wage table. The exact packet, the request and the reply are in `audits/s87-reading/` (`packet.txt`, `request.json`, `response.json`); `read_s87.js` rebuilds the packet.
- **What it was asked:** wrong or imprecise facts; arithmetic or unit errors; wording that is not neutral, overstates, or implies a cause; missing context, especially on the case for and against metal backing; whether Duke's "about 5 ounces of silver a week" was treated fairly.

## F1, the page part (compassionism-simulation `dev/tools/s93_page_check.js`; results `s93-page-check-50bc51d`, PR 80, merged)
The tool renders the page's v5.9 section and its central-bank section (every switch position) twice, from the published data and from the release data, using the same scripts. It pairs every printed figure by file, row label and column. Rows are paired by label, not by position, because some files hold a row only when it meets a rule; the lending study's cost-per-point rows are an example.

- **3,262 figures** are printed in both renders. **2,132** change as printed and **37** change sign. 55 figures print only from the published data and 50 only from the release data, because some lists print a row only when it meets a rule.
- 3,576 rows have a release estimate outside the published 95% interval. This is expected: the main row itself changed.
- 1 main row falls outside the published range across readings: the full-coverage design's programme inflation, Reference, 40 years (0.0655 against 0.0585-0.0648).
- 193 category changes. Of the specification curves, 2 move from fragile to robust: the full-coverage design's financed wealth poverty and programme inflation, Reference, 40 years. The others are 13 automation-study categories that moved from worsens to does-not-offset, and other moves; the lending study's categories (improves, worsens, no change) moved both ways; 10 supply break-even shares became "none on the grid".
- **Sign changes worth stating:**
  - The supply study's reading where wages follow half of programme inflation and the rest is never made up, with nothing backed. At 20 years its effect on basket poverty is now −8.5 (Reference), −12.7 (Adverse) and −1.2 (Stress) points; the published figures were +9.6, +5.0 and +9.6. At 40 years it is +11.3, −2.0 and +12.1, so it is still worse than no programme in Reference and Stress.
  - The yardstick study's change in programme inflation with both readings is now positive (+0.0044 at 20 years in Adverse and Stress). This is the rerun on the engine with the supply-path fix.
  - Smaller items: one financed basket-poverty cell (full coverage, Stress, 20 years: +0.6 to −0.6), and bounds of near-zero intervals.

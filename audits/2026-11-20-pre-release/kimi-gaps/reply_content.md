2. Additional gaps in the findings and limits

- Design inputs are still presented too much like findings. Full backing and full participation produce conditional scenario outputs, not estimates of what the programme will achieve. “Robust” means sign and seed-interval stability within one structure, not robustness to supply, wage setting, backing or engine structure. The page itself reports opt-in wealth harm at no backing, and S82 reports poverty harm with wage lag.

- The denominators need reconciliation: 62 readings versus 48 curves, and the quoted full-coverage range of 25.9–33.3 points alongside Stress at 40.1. Provide a reading-by-curve map showing environment, horizon, backing and screening status.

- Backing and inflation figures are not presented like for like: 0.2–1.9%, 14–20% without the ceiling, and 6–17%/5–18% backing; inflation ranges of 15–53, 18–53, 40–49, 18–71 and 48–69, with 82 at 40 years. Each needs engine, design, environment, horizon, participation, `m`, backing definition and units. Distinguish percentage-point excess inflation from an annual percentage inflation rate. S85’s findings text also omits `m`, contrary to its own stated rule.

- S82’s most unfavourable results are absent from the findings page: no backing can be worse than no programme with wage lag; break-even backing is 0.08–0.26 at 20 years and 0.30–0.49 at 40; BU delays cost 0.7–2.7 points. The design-frequency BU adjustment and wage lag were not run jointly. Moreover, shortages do not enter the money poverty, wealth or inflation measures, so stable money poverty can coexist with unmeasured unmet essentials.

- Financed comparisons are not like for like: contributions are measured against all money income for full coverage but wages for opt-in, and both differ from the full-backing main row. Define “fragile,” keep the omitted weighted top tail beside every affected figure, and do not present debt below GDP as evidence of fiscal sustainability.

- Automation’s preregistered savings result, 66–94%, cannot be compared with the 97–102% basic-living proxy used in sensitivity rows. The trace is a decomposition, not causal evidence. Any automation headline should also retain the 13–24% wealth-offset range with lost jobs and all opt-in harms.

- “Who gains” gives main-row figures without reading ranges or intervals. Persistent poverty among adults outside the labour force belongs in the unfavourable-results section. The spare-cash attribution and the 1.4–7.1 ratio need derivations. Proportional wealth gains are not meaningful from a negative percentile baseline. The labour-share decline is largely an accounting-denominator effect, not evidence of an equal loss in workers’ earnings.

- High-inflation figures need S85’s qualifier that they measure unbacked money under the engine’s rule, not price forecasts. Seed intervals and the 10,000-adult checks are not empirical validation, particularly for poverty and median wealth. The NEEC C2.2 statement needs a source and test basis; assertions that omissions are on the “cautious side” need sensitivity evidence or softer wording.

4. Limits acceptable for this release

Acceptable only with narrowed claims:

- Inv. 1 as an explicit conditional scenario, not a forecast or an achieved-backing result.
- Inv. 2, 4, 12, 13 and 15 as missing mechanisms, provided unbacked poverty and inflation are called model diagnostics, produced backing is called incomplete, and no fiscal, monetary or general-equilibrium forecast is made.
- Inv. 9, 10, 11 and 14, plus fixed population, no migration and omitted care value, if claims are restricted to internal comparisons for the simulated working-age population and no corresponding distributional or spatial claims are made.
- Seed-only intervals, 500 adults and the absence of independent economic review may remain stated limits, but they cannot be accompanied by real-world confidence or validation language.

Not acceptable as mere limits: mixed or selectively rerun engines (Inv. 5); omitted opt-in harms (Inv. 3); live target/validation misinformation (Inv. 6–7); known incorrect figures (Inv. 16); an unreproduced calibration claim (Inv. 18); a confirmed inverted poverty line or missing chart data (Inv. 19–20); and no review of the release artifacts (Inv. 22). Release-relevant specification contradictions and unsupported live citations must likewise be resolved or their claims withdrawn.

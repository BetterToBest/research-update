Before, it fell in 17 cells, rose in the six of 1973-1983, and showed no measurable difference in one. The opt-in design rises in all 24 cells (0.28-0.71), as before.

The comparison direction is hard-coded in `dev/tools/s75_table.js`: the central-bank study's History arm on the engine before the release, where the full-coverage P4 fell. So the 18 new reversals mark a change between engines, not a difference between eras.

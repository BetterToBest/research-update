Below full backing: limits on supply, wages and the BU’s adjustment (v6 readings, added Nov 18, 2026)The v5.9 engine had no limit on what the economy can supply, and wages, the BU and the cost-of-living basket followed prices in full and at once. A preregistered study adds each of these as a reading, behind its own switch, and runs it at every backed share on 500 paired seeds. Full backing is the main row, and no reading changes it. In order of what moves the curve least to most:A limit on supply of housing, medical care and food, from the economy’s headroom in opened sources, shared by the market’s rule, evenly, or the design’s rule. It costs little. With no backing in Reference, the programme’s effect on basket poverty is −30.6 points under the sourced limit with the market’s rule and −28.9 in the strictest case, against −31.0 with no limit. Every primary result holds under the sourced limit at every share, in every environment, with every rule. But the limit as built moves only basket poverty: the money a cut leaves unspent goes to other goods, and the cut moves no price, so wealth, basic living and inflation are the same with and without it.The BU’s adjustment to prices. The design’s monthly and quarterly adjustments cost little; a yearly or two-year delay costs much more (table).Wages that follow programme inflation only in part. This is what makes the results depend on backing. If wages follow half of it and the rest is never made up, the programme with no backing leaves basket poverty +9.6 points against no programme in Reference (+5.0 in Adverse, +9.6 in Stress): worse than no programme.

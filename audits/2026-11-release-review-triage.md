## In short

1. **The four readings agree on the main point, and it holds.** The headline results are conditional on full backing, and the model's poverty effects barely move with backing because the model has no limit on what the economy can supply (in-house finding 1, Nemotron B2-B3, ChatGPT's backing section). Wages follow prices in full (`wIdx` 1), and the Basic Unit and the basket are indexed to the same price level. Below full backing, therefore, the real goods recipients gain are paid for only by holders of savings and lenders. The sentence "the poverty effects barely depend on backing" now carries a dated note saying so (v5.9.11, below). The engine step that adds a limit is S82 (v6).
2. **Two reviewers ask for a backing curve that the release already has.** ChatGPT asks for 0%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% in Reference, Adverse and Stress, with the backed share at which each outcome changes. Nemotron asks for 0%, 20%, 50% and 80%. Question 4's report has exactly ChatGPT's grid in all three environments, at 20 and 40 years, with preregistered break-even shares. What remains is where it is shown. Nemotron would lead the headline cards with a range of backing instead of the main row. Duke decided on Nov 17 that the main row's headlines stay (ledger D32). The narrower question of whether the cards' heading should name the condition is filed as D33 (default: no change, as D32 says).
3. **The documentation has fallen behind the release, and the ODD, test-bed, reproduction and citation files mislead.** ChatGPT's first finding is confirmed in every part. ODD.md says it describes the v5.2 engine. TESTBED_SPEC.md gives the main result as "nothing backed (a = 0)". REPRODUCE.md has no section for v5.6 or v5.9. CITATION.cff says every figure averages 500 paired runs, but the central-bank and history studies use 100. MODEL_SPEC.md has no section on those two studies. v5.9.11 adds a dated "which release this describes" line to each file and corrects the citation's sample size. The rebuild is a new step, S83.
4. **The study runners share no single start, and that caused the central-bank and history studies' error.** Confirmed: 43 of the 71 tools in `dev/tools/` each repeat the two lines that apply the release's global settings. The check added after the error tests only the history study's start. A shared start, a check against the baseline before any study runs, and a record of the effective settings in each run's output make a new step, S84 (tooling only; outputs unchanged).
5. **Smaller confirmed items join the S82 batch as labelled readings.** These are ageing (the main row has no ageing; the 40-year figures should say so), the top tail's fit uncertainty, PTH's equity base, the screen in Stress and on the financed rows, and the wage-only spending share. Several asks are already met: the creditor boundary, the central-bank study's scope, the history study's framing, and the 0.593 spending share as a reading on every curve.

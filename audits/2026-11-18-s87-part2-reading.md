# An outside reading of the later sections of "Money, metals and wages in the US" (S87 part 2, Nov 18, 2026)

Before the Hub page `us-money-history.html` gained its sections on crypto, offshore money and corporate tax, college costs and student debt, media ownership and other changes (S87 items 4-8), a language model from a different maker read the draft, as the plan asks. A model's reading is a check, never a source: nothing it says enters a figure unless the source is opened.

- **Model:** `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b` on NVIDIA's API catalogue (the largest general model in its list on Nov 18), temperature 0.2. The first request, with a 6,000-token limit, spent it all on the model's hidden reasoning and returned no text; the second, with 20,000, answered (12,628 tokens, 11,530 of them reasoning).
- **What it was given:** the five new sections and the page's "What this does not show" section as rendered text, with every table opened, and part 2 of the sources file (`data/s87/SOURCES.md`). The exact packet, the request and the reply are in `audits/s87-part2-reading/` (`packet.txt`, `request.json`, `response.json`); `read_s87_part2.js` rebuilds the packet.
- **What it was asked:** wrong or imprecise facts (dates, amounts, names, laws, court decisions); arithmetic, unit or method errors; wording that is not neutral, overstates the sources, implies an untested cause, or is unfair to any person or company, with particular care for living people and the sitting President; missing context, and counts given without their definitions.

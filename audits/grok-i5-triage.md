Oct 31, 2026 (step S13, first pass; ledger issue I5). Duke asked Grok (Oct 15, 2026) to clone the Simulation repository and "audit the V5.6 Simulation and live HTML pages for clarity, rigor, and technical issues", then suggest improvements. The conversation is shared at https://grok.com/share/c2hhcmQtMw_3ddece2d-1a0d-48e8-8916-7d22a7bbb559 (read this session through its share link; Grok's text is not copied here). This note checks each of Grok's points against the repositories as they stand today (compassionism-simulation `main` at `7b93819`, after S14) and sorts it: **valid, acted on** (with the step that carries it), **already addressed**, or **turned down** (with the reason).

Grok found no correctness bug ("No critical correctness bugs jumped out"). Its findings are about documentation, presentation, structure and research hygiene. Two are valid and matter before the design freeze (S47): the specification documents lag the engine (item 2), and the study programme varies only one reading of how far new output backs the Source's payout (item 6). The rest are already addressed, belong to later steps, or are turned down.

**Why nothing moves.** The borrowed path was the right path. The five settings the key left out change the no-programme run's supply path only in ways these studies did not use: the capacity price rule moves prices only through new money, which a no-programme run has none of (the engine's unit test "no new money" shows the run is identical); the government account's books do not feed back into a no-programme run's demand at the readings swept; bargaining and PTF running costs act through the programme. The reruns show it: the key's fix moves no value in any row of the six studies (PTF realism's one differing row moves for another reason, section 3, and moves the same with or without the fix). Where the defect did move a figure, at the release, it was pay by household type (`eh`), which acts on a no-programme household's earnings; none of these studies has it.

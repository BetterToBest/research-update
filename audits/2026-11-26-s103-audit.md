## 2. The recession-slack reading

The capacity rule's option `rs` counts the share of income a recession leaves idle (after the spending layer) as extra slack. Its unit test expected it to move the full-coverage design's year-20 price level in Adverse seed 39; since v5.9.22 the difference was 1.6e-15, and the v5.9.22 note put that down to the row having taken another row's path before the store was fixed.

**On each row's own path** (`s103_rs.js`, seeds 1-100, three environments; the price-rule study's engine with the store fixed, and the release engine v5.9.23; rows: today's rule, the nesting point with and without the slack, the main values with and without it):
- *The price-rule study's engine.* The reading binds only where a recession leaves idle income: Adverse seed 39 (idle share 0.237) and Stress seeds 39 and 70. There, counting it lowers the nesting point's year-20 level by 0.19% (Adverse) and up to 0.26% (Stress), and at the main values moves it by up to 0.28%; mean effects over 100 seeds are below 0.11 index points on the price level, 0.0004 points on basket poverty and $0.37 on median real wealth. Reference: no seed binds. This is what the price-rule report says (the 0.2% in its tests section, and Amendment 2's account of the first pilot's nesting failure in Adverse and Stress), so its text stands.
- *The release engine.* No seed in 1-100 in any environment has a recession that leaves idle income, so the reading changes nothing.

**Why it changed.** A bisect of seed 39 alone in a fresh process (own path on every engine; `rs39.js`, `rs39.sh`) over the 92 main-line commits from the price-rule study to the release: the idle share is 0.237 on every engine up to `a33c59d` and 0 from `69e8953`, the commit that pays the private businesses' price at the sale. With the old timing (`sp.vAt 'lag'`) the release engines (`69e8953`, `d1f5bf4`, v5.9.23) give exactly the price-rule study's idle share and year-20 level (0.2374403; 1,929.4546). **So the change is the price at the sale, a design change, and not the store.** The v5.9.22 note in the unit test and DECISIONS.md was wrong on the cause. Corrected (compassionism-simulation, this step): the test asserts the reading again at the lagged timing (1.95e-3) and reports the at-sale value as information; DECISIONS.md has a dated S103 entry. The price-rule report gets a dated note giving the release engine's reading; the derivation report states nothing about `rs` and needs none.

- **Cancelled shards (147).** GitHub cancels a job that has waited 24 hours in the queue. With 768 shards dispatched at once on 20 concurrent jobs, the last shards never started (each cancelled at exactly 24 hours after it was queued, Nov 22 23:29 UTC). No cancelled shard had started, so nothing partial exists.
- **Push race (3 runs).** Three automation-study folders (about 1.1 GB each) finished within minutes of each other. The workflow's commit step retries with `git pull --rebase`, but each pull and push of a folder this size took about two minutes, so another folder landed between them every time. Every shard of these runs passed.
- **Recovery.** "Re-run failed jobs" on each run: it reruns only the cancelled or failed jobs, on the same runner commit and workflow (`ed7f35b`), and the finish job collects the shards that already succeeded (their artifacts are kept until Nov 25, about 20:00-24:00 UTC). Seeds are paired, so a shard rerun later gives the same values. **If a run has not landed by Nov 25, its successful shards expire and the whole run must be dispatched again.**
- For later dispatches: send at most about 400 shards at once (20 concurrent jobs at about 30 minutes each finish about 960 in 24 hours, less the checkout time), and dispatch the large automation folders in different hours.
